ProInvoice – Detailed Fictional Description

ProInvoice is a modern, browser‑based invoicing solution designed for freelancers, small business owners, and professionals who need a fast, flexible, and elegant way to create, manage, and send professional invoices. Built with a clean interface and powered entirely by local storage, it gives you full control over your billing data without relying on external servers or subscriptions.

At its core, ProInvoice transforms the tedious task of invoice creation into a seamless, three‑step workflow: set up your reusable business profiles, manage your product/service catalogue, and generate invoices in seconds – all from a single, intuitive dashboard.

---

Key Features That Make ProInvoice Stand Out

1. Smart Business Management

No more typing your company address and tax details every time. With the new Business Management module (accessible from the Settings menu), you can store multiple business profiles – each with a name, address, and a large, multi‑line description field for additional information like GSTIN, phone, email, or bank details.
When you create a new invoice, a dropdown in the “From” section lets you instantly load any saved profile. The details are auto‑filled into the sender area, ensuring consistency and saving you from repetitive data entry. You can add, edit, or delete profiles whenever your business structure changes.

2. Robust Item Catalogue with Editable Descriptions

The Items Management panel lets you build a reusable product/service catalogue. Each item includes a name, a large, multi‑line description box – perfect for detailed specs, terms, or usage notes – and a default rate. Descriptions support line breaks and can be as long as you need.
Unlike static lists, every saved item is fully editable: update the name, description, or rate at any time, and the changes will reflect across all future invoices. You can also delete items you no longer use. The item dropdown appears directly inside the invoice table, so you can quickly select a saved item and have its description and rate automatically filled in.

3. Intelligent Invoice Editor

The invoice editor is where everything comes together. You can:

· Set invoice number, date, due date, and optional PO number.
· Choose from eight distinct layouts (Formal, Corporate, Remittance, Minimal, Elegant, Modern, Soft, Bold) to match your brand identity.
· Add line items with quantity, rate, and auto‑calculated taxable value (based on your CGST/SGST settings).
· Apply discounts – either as a percentage or a fixed flat amount.
· View real‑time subtotal, tax breakdowns, and grand total.
· Add custom notes and terms (which can also be set as default in Settings).

4. Status Tracking & Dashboard

All your invoices are listed on the dashboard with a clear status badge (Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue). You can filter by status or search by invoice number or client name. Each invoice card shows the total amount, date, and client info, with quick Edit and Delete actions. The dashboard also displays the total invoice count, so you always know your billing volume at a glance.

5. Print‑Ready & Professional Output

When you’re ready to send the invoice, ProInvoice offers a print‑optimised view that strips away all editing controls and presents a clean, professional document. You can adjust the print scale (60%–150%) and choose between black‑and‑white (for crisp, high‑contrast printing) or colour (for brand‑rich presentations). A discreet watermark of the invoice number is added to the background for a polished look.

6. Full Data Portability

Your entire data – invoices, settings, items, and business profiles – can be exported as a single JSON file for backup or migration. The import feature lets you restore or transfer your data to another device, making ProInvoice truly portable.

Use Case: A Day in the Life with ProInvoice

Meet Sarah, a freelance graphic designer who runs a small studio. She has three business entities (her own studio, a partnership, and a side consultancy). Each has different tax IDs, addresses, and bank details.
With ProInvoice, Sarah sets up all three businesses in the Business Management section, each with its own description (including GST, PAN, and payment instructions).

She also maintains a catalogue of her design services – logo design, branding kits, social media templates – with detailed descriptions (e.g., “Includes 3 concepts, 2 rounds of revisions, and source files”). The descriptions support multiple lines, so she can add all the terms right there.

When a new client comes in, Sarah clicks New Invoice, selects the appropriate business from the “From” dropdown, fills the client’s details in the “Bill To” area, and picks the relevant service items from the dropdown. The invoice calculates totals, applies the correct taxes (she set default CGST/SGST in Settings), and she adds a personalised note.

She saves the invoice, and it appears on her dashboard. Later, she prints it (or saves as PDF) using the built‑in print function – choosing the elegant layout and colour mode for a premium client presentation.

If she ever needs to update a service description or rate, she simply edits the item in the Items Management panel – and all future invoices will use the updated information.
